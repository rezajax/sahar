**Armenian Grammar Basics**

**Noun Cases**

Armenian has seven noun cases. The ending of a noun changes depending on its role in the sentence.

*   **Nominative:** The subject of the sentence. (e.g., **գիրք** - girk' - book)
*   **Genitive:** Shows possession. (e.g., **գրքի** - grk'i - of the book)
*   **Dative:** The indirect object. (e.g., **գրքին** - grk'in - to the book)
*   **Accusative:** The direct object. (e.g., **գիրքը** - girk'ə - the book)
*   **Ablative:** Shows movement away from something. (e.g., **գրքից** - grk'its' - from the book)
*   **Instrumental:** Shows how something is done. (e.g., **գրքով** - grk'ov - by the book)
*   **Locative:** Shows location. (e.g., **գրքում** - grk'um - in the book)

**Verb Conjugation (Present Tense)**

The verb "to be" (**լինել** - linel) is irregular.

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **եմ** (em) - I am | **ենք** (enk') - we are |
| 2nd | **ես** (es) - you are (inf) | **եք** (ek') - you are (for) |
| 3rd | **է** (ē) - he/she/it is | **են** (en) - they are |

Regular verbs are conjugated by adding endings to the verb stem. For example, the verb "to read" (**կարդալ** - kardal):

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **կարդում եմ** (kardum em) - I read | **կարդում ենք** (kardum enk') - we read |
| 2nd | **կարդում ես** (kardum es) - you read (inf) | **կարդում եք** (kardum ek') - you read (for) |
| 3rd | **կարդում է** (kardum ē) - he/she/it reads | **կարդում են** (kardum en) - they read |

**Sentence Structure**

The basic sentence structure in Armenian is Subject-Object-Verb (SOV).

*   **Ես գիրք եմ կարդում։** (Yes girk' em kardum.) - I am reading a book.
    *   *Subject:* Yes (I)
    *   *Object:* girk' (book)
    *   *Verb:* kardum em (am reading)

**Forming Questions**

To form a question, you can add a question mark at the end of the sentence and raise your intonation. You can also use question words.

*   **Ի՞նչ** (inch') - what
*   **Ո՞վ** (ov) - who
*   **Որտե՞ղ** (vortegh) - where
*   **Ե՞րբ** (yerb) - when
*   **Ինչու՞** (inch'u) - why
*   **Ինչպե՞ս** (inch'pes) - how
