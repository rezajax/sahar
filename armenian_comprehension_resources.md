Here are some resources to help you improve your Armenian comprehension:

**News and Media**

*   **Armenpress:** The oldest Armenian news agency. (https://armenpress.am/arm/)
*   **Armradio:** Public radio with news and other programs. (https://hy.armradio.am/)
*   **News.am:** Popular news outlet with various sections. (https://news.am/arm/)
*   **Tert.am:** Innovative newspaper with a variety of topics. (https://www.tert.am/am/)
*   **Azatutyun:** Provides up-to-date and straightforward breaking news. (https://www.azatutyun.am/)
*   **CivilNet:** Reports on a range of topics relevant to Armenia's latest politics and society. (https://www.civilnet.am/)

**Music**

*   Search for "Armenian music" on YouTube to find a wide variety of genres and artists. Listening to music can help you get used to the sounds and rhythm of the language.

**Reading**

*   **Children's Books:** Start with simple stories to build your vocabulary and confidence.
*   **Short Stories:** Look for Armenian authors and their works online.
*   **News Articles:** Read articles from the news sources listed above.

**Tips for Comprehension**

*   **Start with materials that have transcripts or subtitles.** This will allow you to read along as you listen.
*   **Don't be afraid to listen to or read the same thing multiple times.** Repetition is key to learning.
*   **Focus on understanding the main idea, even if you don't know every word.**
*   **Look up new words and add them to your vocabulary list.**
*   **Be patient!** Improving your comprehension takes time and practice.
